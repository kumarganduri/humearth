# CEO Plan: Hum as the trusted reference (v3)

## Why this phase
Every stage of the v2 roadmap is live (Earth home, /question, /live with live grid carbon, /2030, stage 1b, Hum Daily). What would make Hum fail now is not a missing page:
- **Nobody comes:** there is no distribution beyond share links.
- **Nobody comes back:** Daily is new and unproven.
- **The numbers go stale.**
- **The headline doesn't land:** the 10-second test hasn't been run.

Data sites that last are the ones people reuse. Our World in Data's survey shows its most engaged readers share, download and teach with it, helped by open licences and embeddable charts. Hum's edge is honesty: ranges, verified quotes, calculated values marked. Phase v3 turns that into something others quote, embed and teach with. Every embed and citation links back.

## Step zero (before building): validate (expanded after outside voice)
- **8 to 10 people:** a few everyday readers (the 10-second test from the v2 design doc) plus journalists, teachers and energy researchers. Ask whether they'd cite or embed Hum, what's missing, and where they'd publish.
- **A named list** of outlets, newsletters and communities to pitch, with a one-paragraph pitch each and referral tracking (visit sources in Cloudflare Web Analytics).
- **Division of work:** Hum provides the script and the list; the founder has the conversations.

## Vision
### 10x check
Hum becomes the place a journalist, teacher or policymaker goes when they need "how much electricity does AI use" with a range and a source, and it stays current. Its charts appear in articles and slides, live and attributed. Its data is downloadable and citable, a yearly report gives press a reason to cover it, and it speaks the languages of the places where AI growth meets dirty grids.

## Final shape (after the outside voice)
**Core, built in this order:**
1. **Source register + freshness watch:** a register generated from `data/sources` with a next-review date per source, a quarterly manual review of the sources behind the headline, and the weekly automated quote check as a safety net.
2. **Open data + "cite this":** published only after a per-source licence audit. Each file carries its own terms and attribution. Hum's own words and calculations are CC BY 4.0; quotes stay short cited excerpts. Anything unclear is flagged for the founder (and a lawyer if needed).
3. **Embeds:** live `/embed/*` pages plus an image fallback.
4. **Updates page + RSS/Atom feed:** built from the changelog.
5. **Press and teacher kit.**
6. **"Why we can't tell you per model" explainer:** replaces the "Which AI do you use?" picker. A few sourced examples, each with its boundary, shown individually with no comparison.

**Gated extras:** each keeps its approved design and is built only when its unlock signal appears (see TODOS.md).
- **Email digest:** readers ask for it, or the feed gets subscribers.
- **More live hubs (ENTSO-E):** a defined user question they answer, plus an owner for outages.
- **Annual "State of AI's footprint":** when the press list shows interest, or at the first year-end with new IEA/Ember editions.
- **Translations:** a fluent reviewer is lined up, and real traffic comes from that language's countries.

**Immediately after this review (live-site honesty fix):** reword the /live grid cards. "One AI answer's electricity here, right now" becomes "If one AI answer ran on this grid's average electricity", plus a note that data centres may buy cleaner or dirtier power.

## Requirements from the review (each approved)
1. **Embed security:** only `/embed/*` may be framed. Those pages replace (not add to) the site-wide CSP, keep every other rule, and contain nothing that changes state. Tests prove `/embed/*` loads in a frame and every other page refuses.
2. **Email privacy:** double opt-in; open and click tracking off at the provider; an unsubscribe link in every email; at most monthly. The How We Know privacy section is updated before the sign-up form goes live. Hum's pages still load nothing from the provider.
3. **Per-model honesty** (now applies to the explainer's examples; the picker was cut by OV4): each model's figure carries its boundary (chips only, plus servers, or plus building) and who measured it. Nothing is ranked across different boundaries, and "not published" falls back to Hum's general range. The build rejects a per-model figure without a boundary.
4. **Translations:** all text lives in per-language files. The build fails on missing keys and flags strings whose English changed since translation (shown in English with "translation pending"). Quotes stay in their original language, and each language goes live only after a fluent reviewer signs off.
5. **Freshness watch:** each source has three outcomes. Quote found is fine. Quote missing on a page that loaded opens or updates one GitHub issue. Page blocked or down goes in a weekly summary, with an issue only after three weeks down. It never blocks deploys, and it's tested with saved pages.
6. **Embed weight:** the race, fan and /2030 embeds are prebuilt SVG plus a little script, under 30 KB. The globe embed is a still image until tapped. CI enforces a size budget for `/embed/*`.

## Not in scope
- Accounts or logins on Hum.
- Ads or tracking beyond Cloudflare's cookieless visit counts.
- A browser extension that counts personal AI use (privacy surface; revisit after v3).
- Ranking AI models or companies against each other.
- A per-model picker (replaced by the explainer; revisit when per-model disclosure becomes standard).
