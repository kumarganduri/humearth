# Hum question bank (2026-10-18)

438 questions from 50 research angles, merged into 310 distinct questions. Each figure's quote was re-opened on its page by a separate checker: 1,683 of 1,688 found (119 with minor wording differences such as footnote markers), 5 failed (listed at the end). Full data, sources and checks: `question-bank.json`.

Priority is how likely an ordinary visitor is to ask it (5 = most). Verdicts are the researchers' drafts, not final copy. Nothing here is in `data/sources` yet.

## About you (23)

- **[5] How much electricity does one ChatGPT question use, and does a longer question use more?** A short question uses a fraction of a watt-hour. Pasting in a long document can multiply that 10 to 100 times. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q002)_
- **[5] Should I (or my kids) stop using ChatGPT to help the planet?** One student's homework prompts are small next to a car trip. The concern is the total across everyone, which is growing fast. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q011, q412)_
- **[4] How much does my own ChatGPT use add to my carbon footprint in a year?** For a typical user, about 0.2 kg of CO2 a year, a tiny share of a personal footprint of several tonnes. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q004)_
- **[4] Do 'thinking' or reasoning modes like GPT-5 or o3 use more energy than normal answers, and why?** Yes, much more. In tests on open models, reasoning used about 30 times more energy on average. _(6 drafts, 20 sourced figures; q006, q045, q053, q054, q056, q414)_
- **[4] Which uses more energy: training the AI or people using it?** Training one model is a big one-off cost, but answering everyone's questions usually uses more energy in total. _(4 drafts, 14 sourced figures; q007, q020, q070, q075)_
- **[4] How much CO2 does one ChatGPT question make?** Around a sixth of a gram of CO2 on an average grid, though it depends on where the data centre gets its power. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q010)_
- **[4] Are AI pictures and videos much worse for the planet than text chats?** Yes. Images use a few times more than a text answer, and short AI videos can use thousands of times more. _(2 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q017, q417)_
- **[4] Do Google's AI Overviews make every search use more energy?** Almost certainly some more, but Google has not published how much, and outside estimates disagree widely. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q088)_
- **[4] Is it greener to use a smaller 'mini' or 'flash' AI model?** Generally yes. In open-model tests, a much larger model used about 59 times the energy per answer. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q415)_
- **[3] Does choosing low reasoning or turning thinking off actually save energy?** Yes, in the tests published so far. The size of the saving depends heavily on the model. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q055)_
- **[3] How much energy does chatting with an AI companion for hours use?** Companion apps publish no energy figures. Using OpenAI's average, 100 messages is less than an hour of video streaming. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q016)_
- **[3] Does pasting long documents or keeping long chats use more energy?** Yes. A very long input can take roughly 10 to 100 times the energy of a short question, though mostly as a one-off cost. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q058, q416)_
- **[3] How much electricity does an AI agent or coding agent like Claude Code use?** Far more than a chat question: estimates run from tens to hundreds of Wh per session and 1 to 6 kWh for a heavy day. _(2 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q059, q060)_
- **[3] Is it greener (and does it save water) to run AI on my phone instead of the cloud?** Not clearly. Studies disagree, and the answer depends on whether the cloud servers are well run. _(2 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q079, q086)_
- **[3] Does on-phone AI like Apple Intelligence drain my battery?** Roughly a quarter to three-quarters of 1% of a full charge per answer, in one lab test. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q080, q081)_
- **[3] Can I turn off Google's AI Overviews, and does it save energy?** You can't switch them off, but the 'Web' filter hides them. We could not find a published energy figure for one AI Overview. _(1 drafts, 1 sourced figures; q420)_
- **[3] My electricity supplier says my tariff is 100% renewable. Is that the same trick?** Often the same mechanism: annual certificate matching. Measured hour by hour, some '100% renewable' tariffs come out much lower. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q216)_
- **[3] Does the time of day I use AI change its carbon footprint?** In principle yes, but you can't see or choose which data centre or grid serves you, so it's not a practical lever today. _(1 drafts, 1 sourced figures; q421)_
- **[2] Are reasoning models worth the extra energy?** Often more accurate, but at a much higher footprint per answer. The trade-off depends on the task. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q057)_
- **[2] How much electricity does it take to make a song with AI?** For the open music models that have been measured, it ranges from about one image's worth of energy to a few images' worth per 30-second clip. Suno and Udio publish nothing. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q065)_
- **[2] How much electricity did the Ghibli-style AI image trend use?** Nobody published the real figure. A rough estimate is hundreds to a couple of thousand megawatt-hours: a tiny slice of world data-centre use, though large for one feature in one week. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q066)_
- **[2] Does Apple Intelligence run on my phone or in Apple's data centres?** Both. A small model runs on the device, and harder requests go to Apple's servers. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q082)_
- **[2] Does running a chatbot on my own gaming PC use more electricity than ChatGPT?** Usually yes, per answer. A home graphics card serves only you, and data centres share hardware across many users. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q083)_

## Comparisons and myths (32)

- **[5] Does asking ChatGPT really use 10 times more energy than a Google search?** Probably not any more. The '10x' compares an old ChatGPT estimate with a 2009 Google figure. _(5 drafts, 24 sourced figures; q001, q022, q035, q087, q113)_
- **[5] Does ChatGPT really use a bottle of water for every email or question?** No. The study behind it said one bottle per 10 to 50 answers, counting power-station water, for an older model. _(7 drafts, 28 sourced figures; q003, q012, q021, q036, q114, q115, q129)_
- **[5] Is asking ChatGPT worse than streaming Netflix or YouTube?** No. An hour of streaming used about as much as a couple of hundred short ChatGPT questions. _(6 drafts, 26 sourced figures; q009, q013, q032, q089, q111, q419)_
- **[4] Does saying 'please' and 'thank you' to ChatGPT waste energy or cost millions?** A few extra words cost almost nothing. A separate 'thank you' message costs about as much as any other short message. _(5 drafts, 11 sourced figures; q005, q014, q037, q118, q413)_
- **[4] How much electricity does one big AI data centre use compared with homes?** A typical AI-focused data centre uses as much as about 100,000 households, and the biggest ones being built will use about 20 times that. _(3 drafts, 11 sourced figures; q027, q303, q354)_
- **[4] Does making one AI image use as much energy as charging my phone?** Usually not. A typical image uses a few percent of a phone charge. The comparison comes from the least efficient model in a 2023 test. _(2 drafts, 10 sourced figures; q062, q119)_
- **[4] Will deleting old emails and photos really save water?** There is no evidence it saves a meaningful amount, and deleting things uses a little energy too. _(3 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q092, q117, q418)_
- **[4] Is one AI chatbot question really like boiling a kettle?** No. By the companies' own figures, one text prompt uses a few hundred times less energy than boiling a litre of water. _(2 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q104, q105)_
- **[4] Is using AI worse for the climate than taking a flight?** For ordinary chatting, no. One domestic flight emits as much as a couple of million text prompts. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q107)_
- **[3] Will AI use a quarter of all US electricity by 2030?** Not supported: a US government lab projects all data centres at 6.7% to 12% of US electricity by 2028, with AI only part of that. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q038)_
- **[3] Does training one AI model emit as much CO2 as five cars over their lifetimes?** Misleading: the 'five cars' figure was for a rare, extreme model-design search in 2019, not for training a typical model, and later Google research said that estimate was too high. _(2 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q040, q120)_
- **[3] Does AI use more electricity than bitcoin mining?** All data centres together use about three times Bitcoin's electricity; whether AI alone has overtaken Bitcoin is unknown, because AI's share is not published. _(3 drafts, 14 sourced figures; q041, q095, q096)_
- **[3] Is DeepSeek greener than ChatGPT, and did its efficiency mean less energy use?** Cheaper to train, yes, but its reasoning model may use more energy per answer, and DeepSeek publishes no energy data. _(3 drafts, 13 sourced figures; q049, q283, q389)_
- **[3] How many AI prompts equal charging my phone?** A full phone charge is about 19 Wh, roughly 55 to 80 text prompts on the companies' own figures. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q112)_
- **[3] Is a video call with my camera on bad for the climate?** The estimates disagree by more than tenfold. The most-quoted study gives a high range, and IEA figures for video streaming are far lower. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q090)_
- **[3] What is the carbon footprint of an email, and should I stop sending thank-you emails?** A short email's footprint is tiny, and the famous '4 g per email' comes from a 2010 back-of-envelope estimate that its own author has since cut. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q091)_
- **[3] Does gaming use more electricity than AI?** Per person, a gaming PC uses far more electricity than a typical chatbot user. In total, AI data centres are bigger and growing much faster. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q093)_
- **[3] How does the CO2 from an AI prompt compare with driving a car one kilometre?** Driving one kilometre in an average US car emits about as much CO2 as a couple of thousand text prompts. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q106)_
- **[3] How does a beef burger compare with AI in carbon terms?** 100 g of beef from a beef herd is roughly 6 kg CO2e, about as much as tens of thousands of text prompts. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q108)_
- **[3] Is watching 30 minutes of Netflix really like driving 4 miles?** No. The headline rested on a bits-versus-bytes slip, and the IEA estimated the real impact was up to about 90 times smaller. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q121)_
- **[2] Is AI more energy-efficient than a human brain?** A brain runs on about 20 watts. A single AI answer uses little, but no one has a fair like-for-like comparison. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q015)_
- **[2] Were data centres really going to use 1,000 TWh, as much as Japan, by 2026?** Misread: the 2024 figure bundled crypto mining with data centres and was a high-end projection; the IEA's later data put data centres at 485 TWh in 2025, with Japan-scale use expected around 2030. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q039)_
- **[2] Is one bitcoin transaction worse than thousands of ChatGPT questions?** On paper one transaction equals millions of prompts, but per-transaction is a shaky way to measure bitcoin. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q097)_
- **[2] Is bitcoin mining greener or dirtier than AI data centres?** Unclear. Miners say about half their power is renewable or nuclear, and estimates of bitcoin's CO2 range from 40 to 114 Mt. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q098)_
- **[2] Is AI art better for the climate than a human artist drawing it?** One study says AI emits hundreds to thousands of times less CO2 per picture. The result depends on counting the human's whole daily footprint, so it is a framing choice more than a direct measurement. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q067)_
- **[2] Is cloud gaming worse for the planet than a console or PC?** Usually yes. Berkeley Lab found that cloud gaming adds hundreds of watts per player in data centres and networks. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q094)_
- **[2] How much electricity does a fridge use compared with AI prompts?** US households use roughly 600 to 900 kWh a year for refrigeration. One day of that equals thousands of text prompts. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q110)_
- **[2] Is streaming TV worse than broadcast TV, and is using AI at work better than doing it by hand?** In the government study, streaming used more electricity than broadcast TV. AI-assisted office tasks used less than doing them by hand. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q312)_
- **[2] Is it true the data centre industry supports 4.7 million US jobs?** It is an industry-funded model that includes indirect and spending-driven jobs. It is not a count of people who work inside data centres. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q344)_
- **[2] Weren't data centres supposed to use a fifth of the world's electricity by 2025?** No. That 2017 forecast was for the whole communications sector, not just data centres, and data centres ended up around 1.5% of world electricity. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q361)_
- **[2] Does AI weather forecasting use less energy than normal forecasts?** Yes. Once trained, an AI forecast uses about 1,000 times less energy to run. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q383)_
- **[1] Didn't Ethereum fix crypto's energy problem, and could AI do something similar?** Ethereum cut its electricity use by about 99.99% by dropping mining. AI has no equivalent switch. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q102)_

## The world's total (12)

- **[5] What share of the world's electricity do data centres and AI use?** About 1.5% of world electricity in 2024, and most of it is used in the US and China. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q026, q422)_
- **[4] How many homes could the world's data centres power, and how do they compare with households?** About 39 million average US homes' worth of electricity in 2024, and far more homes in countries that use less per house. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q109, q428)_
- **[4] How many data centres are there in the world?** No one keeps an official global count. Directories list thousands of data centres, and their numbers disagree. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q348)_
- **[4] What share of global CO2 emissions comes from data centres and AI?** About half of one percent of energy-related CO2. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q423)_
- **[3] What does everyone's ChatGPT use add up to?** Answering text chats alone comes to roughly 0.3 TWh a year, well under 1% of data-centre electricity. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q008)_
- **[3] How much electricity and CO2 does it take to train a big AI model like GPT-3 or GPT-4?** About 1,287 MWh and 552 tonnes CO2e for GPT-3 in 2020, roughly three round trips of a whole passenger jet across the US. Newer models have not published theirs. _(4 drafts, 17 sourced figures; q019, q033, q071, q072)_
- **[3] Do data centres and AI pollute more than flying?** No. Aviation emits roughly five times as much CO2 as all data centres today. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q424)_
- **[3] Which country has the most data centres?** The United States, by a wide margin, by count and by capacity. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q349)_
- **[3] What is the biggest AI data centre in the world?** By Epoch AI's satellite-based estimate it is Colossus 2 in Memphis, drawing about 946 MW of IT power. That title changes every few months. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q351)_
- **[2] What is a hyperscale data centre, and how many are there?** Hyperscale means the giant data centres run by the biggest cloud and internet companies. There are about 1,360, and they hold nearly half the world's capacity. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q350)_
- **[2] How do AI's emissions compare with steel and cement?** Steel and cement each emit more than ten times as much CO2 as all data centres. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q425)_
- **[2] Does AI use more electricity than air conditioning?** No. Air conditioning uses about six times as much electricity as all data centres. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q426)_

## Growth and the future (34)

- **[4] Could AI cut more emissions than it causes?** Possibly, but only on paper so far. The IEA says AI-led savings could reach about 5% of energy emissions by 2035 if widely used, and calls AI 'not a silver bullet'. _(3 drafts, 11 sourced figures; q024, q378, q430)_
- **[4] Is AI the main reason electricity demand from data centres and the world is growing?** AI servers are the fastest-growing part of data-centre use. Globally, though, data centres are only about a tenth of new electricity demand. _(3 drafts, 17 sourced figures; q030, q360, q427)_
- **[4] If AI keeps getting more efficient, why is its electricity use still going up?** Each answer uses far less energy than it used to, but people now ask for far more answers, and heavier kinds of answers. _(3 drafts, 12 sourced figures; q387, q394, q395)_
- **[3] How much of America's expected electricity growth is because of data centres?** About half: utility forecasts add roughly 166 GW of US peak demand by 2030, and data centres account for about 55% of that. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q372)_
- **[3] Could data centres use half of Virginia's electricity by 2030?** Only in EPRI's highest scenario: 46% by 2030. Its scenarios range from 29% to 46%. _(2 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q298, q374)_
- **[3] How big is OpenAI's Stargate, really?** Stargate is planned at over 9 GW across seven US sites, but only Abilene was running by mid-2026, at a few hundred megawatts. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q352)_
- **[3] How fast are giant AI data centres growing?** Very fast. The biggest site's computing power doubles roughly every seven months, and AI-factory capacity more than tripled in 18 months. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q356)_
- **[3] How much electricity will US data centres use by 2030?** Roughly two to three times today's level, but the range is wide: about 325–580 TWh by 2028 (LBNL) or about 196–404 TWh by 2030 (EPRI). _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q367)_
- **[3] If AI gets cheaper and more efficient, won't people just use more (the Jevons paradox)?** It is a real risk that researchers and the IEA both raise. No one has measured its size yet. _(3 drafts, 10 sourced figures; q385, q388, q393)_
- **[2] Why are bitcoin mines turning into AI data centres?** Because the scarce thing for AI is a ready grid connection, and miners already have one. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q099)_
- **[2] How much power does a frontier AI training run draw, and is it growing?** The biggest runs now draw over 100 MW. Epoch AI forecasts 4-16 GW per run by 2030, a projection rather than a measurement. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q076)_
- **[2] Will AI on phones and laptops mean we need fewer data centres?** Some, but nobody has put a number on it, and the forecasts still show data centres growing fast. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q084)_
- **[2] How much of PJM's expected demand growth is from data centres?** Almost all of it, according to PJM's own forecast, and the market monitor says data centres are the main reason capacity prices rose. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q155)_
- **[2] How much extra electricity demand are data centres adding to North American grids?** NERC's 2025 outlook expects about 224 GW more summer peak demand within a decade, mostly from data centres. That is a forecast. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q163)_
- **[2] How much bigger will data-centre electricity use get in Ireland?** EirGrid forecasts data centres and other big tech loads at about 32% of Ireland's electricity demand by 2030, with up to 6 GW more interest waiting. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q255)_
- **[2] How much electricity will Europe's data centres use by 2030?** Ember projects Europe's data centres will go from 96 TWh in 2024 to 168 TWh in 2030 and 236 TWh in 2035. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q373)_
- **[2] Could data centres use 30% of Malaysia's electricity by 2030?** It is one forecast (Ember): 9 TWh in 2024 rising to 68 TWh in 2030. It depends on whether pipeline projects actually get built and fill up. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q275)_
- **[2] How fast is data-centre electricity growing in Southeast Asia?** The IEA expects it to roughly double by 2030, from a small base of about 3% of the world's data-centre demand. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q278)_
- **[2] How fast will China's data-centre electricity use grow?** The IEA forecasts that it will grow about 2.7-fold by 2030, adding around 175 TWh. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q281)_
- **[2] How much data centre capacity does the UK want by 2030, and could the grid cope?** The government wants at least 6 GW by 2030, three times today's capacity. The grid operator says that much is manageable. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q306)_
- **[2] How much will AI add to the UK's carbon emissions?** The government's model gives 34 to 123 million tonnes of CO2 over 2025 to 2035, about 1 to 3% of UK emissions. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q311)_
- **[2] How big is Meta's Hyperion data centre in Louisiana?** Meta says 5 GW and more than $50 billion when finished around 2032. Independent estimates for early 2028 are about 1.6 GW of IT power. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q353)_
- **[2] Why didn't data-centre electricity grow much in the 2010s despite more internet use?** Efficiency gains kept pace with demand: better chips, fuller servers, and a move from small server rooms to big cloud sites. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q358, q390)_
- **[2] When did data-centre electricity use start rising again, and why?** Around 2017, when the number of servers began growing again and GPU servers for AI became a noticeable share of the fleet. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q359)_
- **[2] What do banks like Goldman Sachs predict for data-centre power by 2030?** Goldman Sachs Research forecasts global data centre power demand up to 165% above 2023 levels by 2030. That is a bank's forecast, measured in gigawatts, not in electricity used over a year. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q370)_
- **[2] What is BloombergNEF's forecast for US data-centre power demand?** 106 GW of US data centre power demand by 2035, revised up 36% in just seven months. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q371)_
- **[2] How big could AI data centres get in the US by 2035?** Deloitte estimates AI data centres in the US could go from 4 GW in 2024 to 123 GW by 2035, a consultancy forecast at the high end. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q377)_
- **[2] How can AI help the electricity grid?** By squeezing more out of the wires already in place and forecasting wind and solar better. The gains are potential, not counted. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q380)_
- **[2] Can AI discover new materials for batteries and solar panels?** It is speeding up the search. Few AI-found materials have been made in a lab, and none has yet been shown to cut emissions. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q382)_
- **[2] Can AI cut emissions from flights and traffic?** Small trials look promising, but every result so far comes from Google itself. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q386)_
- **[2] Will new chips like Nvidia's Blackwell cut AI's power use?** Each chip does much more work per unit of energy, but so far that has meant more AI for the power used, not less power in total. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q391)_
- **[1] What is Koomey's law, and can efficiency keep cancelling out growth?** It is the long trend of computers doing more work per unit of energy; it has slowed since 2000, and the IEA warned it may slow further. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q362)_
- **[1] Did experts see the AI electricity surge coming?** Mostly not: forecasts made around 2016-2019 expected flat use, though some researchers warned a sharp rise was possible. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q363)_
- **[1] Has data-centre electricity use surged like this before?** Yes. US data-centre electricity roughly doubled from 2000 to 2005, then levelled off for about a decade. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q365)_

## Your bills and the grid (24)

- **[5] Are data centres making my electricity bill go up?** In the PJM grid region, the official market monitor says data centres are the main reason capacity costs jumped, and those costs flow into bills; how much your bill changes depends on your utility and state. _(2 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q043, q347)_
- **[4] Are data centres pushing up electricity bills in the PJM region of the eastern US?** Partly yes: PJM's independent market monitor estimates data-centre demand has added tens of billions of dollars to wholesale capacity costs, and those costs reach household bills. _(2 drafts, 10 sourced figures; q140, q149)_
- **[4] Will data centres keep pushing bills up in the future?** Forecasts point up, but how far depends on how much forecast demand actually arrives and on new rules about who pays. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q146)_
- **[3] How much have household electricity prices gone up in Virginia, Maryland, Ohio and Pennsylvania?** In the year to July 2026, residential prices in these states rose 11-14%, more than double the US average of about 5%. Data centres are one cause among several. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q141)_
- **[3] Is Dominion raising Virginia bills because of data centres?** Bills are going up (by about $13.60 a month by 2027 from base rates alone), and regulators have created a separate rate class so very large users such as data centres carry more of their own costs from 2027. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q143)_
- **[3] Are Ohio households paying for the data-centre boom?** Ohio's regulator now requires big new data centres to pay for most of the capacity they reserve, to stop the costs moving onto other customers. Ohio residential prices still rose about 12% in the year to July 2026. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q144, q301)_
- **[3] Do tech companies get secret electricity deals that shift costs onto ordinary customers?** It is possible but not proven case by case. Researchers warn that confidential contracts could shift costs, and regulators in Virginia and Ohio have responded with large-load rules. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q145)_
- **[3] Are data centres making Irish electricity bills go up?** Irish household prices are the EU's highest (measured). How much of that is caused by data centres has not been quantified in any source we could verify. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q147, q257)_
- **[3] Will AI data centres put up UK household energy bills?** Unknown for now: we found no official UK estimate of data centres' effect on household bills. _(2 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q148, q308)_
- **[3] Who pays for the power lines built for new data centres?** In most PJM states studied, ordinary customers do, through their bills. _(2 drafts, 7 sourced figures; q151, q154)_
- **[3] Have data centres caused a blackout in Virginia, and why is it a problem if they suddenly switch off?** No: there has been no blackout so far. But data centres have suddenly dropped off the grid in large blocks, and grid operators treat that as a new risk. _(2 drafts, 10 sourced figures; q157, q158)_
- **[3] Is the Texas grid going to fail because of data centres?** Not so far. ERCOT met a record 91.1 GW peak in July 2026 without emergencies, but requests to connect are several times the size of today's grid. _(1 drafts, 7 sourced figures; q159)_
- **[3] Will data centres cause blackouts in the PJM region?** There's no shortage yet, but a state-commissioned study finds PJM would miss its reliability target by 2030 in every scenario that includes new data centres. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q162)_
- **[3] Could data centres cause blackouts in Ireland?** There have been no data-centre-caused blackouts, but data centres now use over a fifth of Ireland's metered electricity, which is why the grid operator has limited new connections around Dublin. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q164, q256)_
- **[2] Do bitcoin miners help the grid by switching off, and could AI data centres do the same?** Miners do switch off at peaks and get paid for it. AI data centres have rarely done so at scale so far. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q100)_
- **[2] How much does the PJM capacity auction add to a household's monthly bill?** Roughly $10 to $21 a month in total from the last two auctions, by the consumer advocate's estimate, with data-centre demand named as the main driver. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q142)_
- **[2] How much have data centres added to PJM capacity costs in total?** About $21 billion over three auctions, according to the market monitor's estimate. _(1 drafts, 1 sourced figures; q156)_
- **[2] How long is the queue to connect new power plants to the US grid?** Huge and slow: about 2,300 GW is waiting, a typical project now takes over four years, and most never get built. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q152)_
- **[2] Can data centres help the grid by using less power at peak times?** Some can, and a few now do, but so far as small pilots and company announcements rather than proven at scale. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q165)_
- **[2] Will data centres cause power cuts in India?** Not on current evidence. Air conditioning and industry drive India's demand growth, and data centres are a small part. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q291)_
- **[1] Why do AI training runs worry grid operators?** Because their power use is huge, concentrated in one place, and can swing sharply within minutes. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q077)_
- **[1] Why does the PJM capacity auction keep clearing at its price cap?** Demand forecasts driven by data centres are rising faster than new supply, so the auction hits its ceiling and still comes up short. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q150)_
- **[1] Are data centres clogging up the UK grid connection queue?** Yes. Data centres have asked for about 73 GW of connections, more than Britain's whole peak demand of about 45 GW. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q307)_
- **[1] Can data centres run diesel generators to help the grid in heatwaves?** Normally only a little, but in a declared emergency the US government has let them go over their permits. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q326)_

## Water (22)

- **[4] How much water do US data centres use in total?** About 17 billion gallons on-site in 2023, plus roughly 12 times that indirectly through the power plants that make their electricity. Both are estimates. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q134)_
- **[4] Do data centres use drinking water?** Mostly, yes. Over three-quarters of what Google's data centres drew in 2024 was potable water, though some sites run on reclaimed water. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q136)_
- **[3] How much water does it take to make one AI image?** Probably a few millilitres, most of it at the power station rather than the data centre. Nobody has measured it directly. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q068)_
- **[3] Are data centres using up Arizona's water?** Not today by volume: farms use most of Arizona's water, and data-centre cooling is a small slice. But it is growing, and much of the data is hidden. _(1 drafts, 7 sourced figures; q122)_
- **[3] How much water do Texas data centres use, and could they run Texas dry?** About 0.4% of Texas water use in 2025, according to one independent estimate. That could rise to 2.7% by 2030, and the use is clustered in dry areas. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q123)_
- **[3] Is most of a data centre's water used at the building or at the power plant?** In Texas, about two-thirds of it is at the power plants, according to HARC's estimate. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q124)_
- **[3] Can data centres be built to use no water at all?** For cooling, mostly yes, but it costs more electricity, and the power plants still use water. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q130, q133)_

## Policy and rules (25)

- **[3] Is there an energy rating for data centres or AI models, like on fridges?** A common EU rating with electronic labels was proposed in September 2026. Minimum performance standards may follow, with a proposal planned for 2027. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q400, q410)_
- **[3] Are any places banning or pausing new data centres?** Several US states proposed moratoriums in 2026, and Maine came within one vote, but none passed according to the tracker. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q336)_
- **[2] Should schools stop students using AI because of climate change?** Homework prompts are a tiny slice of a fast-growing total. The data cannot say how big the education slice is. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q018)_
- **[2] Does the law make data centres reuse their waste heat (EU, Germany)?** Partly. Big data centres must use their waste heat unless they can show it isn't technically or economically feasible. _(3 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q248, q249, q436)_
- **[2] Can Texas switch off data centres in a power emergency, and what did SB 6 do?** Yes. Under Texas law (SB 6), new large loads of 75 MW or more must be able to be cut off during rolling outages, and ERCOT can order them onto backup generators. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q160, q402)_
- **[2] Why did Texas pause new data centres in 2026?** Texas paused new grid connections and state permits for data centres while it audits what is really being built and checks the effect on power and water. _(1 drafts, 4 sourced figures; q161)_

- **[2] Do data centres have to report their energy and water use, and can the public see it?** Data centres of 500 kW or more must report energy and water data every year, but the public only sees national and EU totals. _(3 drafts, 13 sourced figures; q267, q396, q408)_
- **[2] Is there a ban on new data centres in Dublin, and what are Ireland's new grid rules?** There is no formal ban, but since late 2021 new Dublin grid connections have been nearly impossible, and Dublin is still treated as constrained. _(2 drafts, 7 sourced figures; q253, q254)_
- **[2] Did the Netherlands or Amsterdam ban new data centres?** Yes, for very large ones: since 2022 they are only allowed in two designated areas. _(2 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q262, q263)_
- **[2] Is the EU trying to limit data centres or build more?** Both: the EU wants to at least triple capacity while adding a rating scheme and, later, minimum performance standards. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q270)_
- **[2] Does the EU AI Act make AI companies disclose their models' energy use?** Partly. Providers of large AI models must record their models' energy use, but it goes to the EU's AI Office on request and is not published. _(2 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q399, q409)_
- **[2] Why did Singapore pause new data centres, and is there a limit now?** Singapore paused new data centres from 2019 to 2022 to work out how to grow sustainably. It now gives out capacity through competitive calls, and has not published a hard cap. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q272)_
- **[2] How much money do states give up in data centre tax breaks, and per job?** Billions a year, and the cost is rising fast. In four states it is now over $1 billion a year each. _(2 drafts, 12 sourced figures; q340, q341)_
- **[2] Do states earn back the tax they give up on data centres?** Not at state level. The three states that measured it got back less than they gave up, though local governments can come out ahead. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q342)_

- **[2] Can I find out which companies get data centre tax breaks in my state?** Usually not. Most states with these programmes do not name the companies that benefit. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q346)_
- **[1] Why did regulators stop Amazon getting more power straight from a nuclear plant?** US regulators rejected an expanded direct hookup in 2024, after utilities argued other customers could end up paying more. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q171)_
- **[1] What is a DOE emergency order, and who pays to keep coal plants running?** The US Energy Department used an emergency power-law clause to order plants to stay available, and the costs fall on electricity customers. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q185)_
- **[1] Does hourly matching cut more emissions than annual matching, and what does it cost?** Models say yes once the hourly match passes about 85%, and the cost rises steeply near 100%. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q195)_
- **[1] Will companies be required to match clean energy hour by hour?** It has been proposed but not decided. Most respondents, especially companies, did not support the draft as written. _(2 drafts, 11 sourced figures; q196, q215)_

- **[1] What rules does Germany set for data centres' efficiency and green power?** Germany's Energy Efficiency Act caps energy overhead, requires 100% renewable electricity from 2027, and makes new centres reuse some waste heat. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q266)_

## Hard to know (10)

- **[3] Why do estimates of water per AI prompt differ so much (5 drops vs 45 mL)?** They measure different things. The 170-fold gap mostly reflects what each company chose to count. _(2 drafts, 9 sourced figures; q051, q116)_
- **[3] Do independent researchers agree with AI companies' per-question numbers?** For short text questions, roughly yes, at a few tenths of a watt-hour. Long or reasoning answers can be many times higher. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q052)_
- **[3] Are data-centre demand forecasts inflated by speculative projects?** Some are. When AEP Ohio made data centres commit financially, its projected data-centre demand fell by more than half. _(2 drafts, 7 sourced figures; q153, q369)_
- **[3] Why do different reports give different numbers for the same year?** They count different things (with or without crypto mining) and revise old estimates as better data arrives. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q364)_
- **[3] Why do data-centre electricity forecasts disagree so much?** Nobody measures this well today, and the future depends on chip shipments, how hard the chips are run, cooling and AI demand, so serious forecasters publish ranges rather than single numbers. _(1 drafts, 6 sourced figures; q368)_
- **[2] How much energy does one AI video use, and does length or resolution matter?** Because the model, the resolution, the length and the hardware all differ. The same study measured 0.14 Wh for a tiny model and over 415 Wh for a big one. _(2 drafts, 8 sourced figures; q063, q064)_
- **[2] What does a '5-gigawatt data centre' actually mean?** It usually means the most power the site is built or connected to draw, not what it actually uses. Figures often mix chip power with whole-site power. _(1 drafts, 5 sourced figures; q355)_
- **[2] What's the difference between a forecast in gigawatts and one in terawatt-hours?** GW is how much power is drawn at once; TWh is how much electricity is used over a year. Converting between them requires knowing how hard the equipment runs. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q375)_
- **[2] Where does the claim that AI can cut 5-10% of global emissions come from?** It is a company-commissioned estimate, not a measurement. _(1 drafts, 3 sourced figures; q379)_
- **[1] Do official statistics count the gas plants data centres build for themselves?** Often not: the EIA planned-capacity totals cover grid-connected plants, so on-site gas is undercounted. _(1 drafts, 2 sourced figures; q180)_
