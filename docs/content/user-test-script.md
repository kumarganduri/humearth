# Hum: 10-minute phone test

**Goal:** find out whether a first-time visitor understands what Hum is for in 10 seconds, stays past a minute, and would send something to someone.

**People:** 5 to 10, as mixed as you can manage, for example:
- someone who uses ChatGPT a lot;
- someone who doesn't;
- a teenager;
- a parent;
- someone outside the US or UK;
- someone who reads the news closely (teacher, journalist).

Not other builders. One person at a time.

**You need:** your phone on **humearth.org** (the front door, not an answer page), and this sheet.

---

## Before you start (say this)
> "I'm testing a website, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud: say what you're looking at and what you expect. I won't help, because I want to see where it's unclear."

## 1. First 10 seconds (don't touch anything)
Hand them the phone on the front door. Start a timer. Say nothing for 10 seconds, then ask:
- **"What is this site for?"**
- **"Who do you think it's for?"**

Write down their words exactly. Don't correct them.

## 2. Free use (2 minutes)
> "Use it however you like for two minutes, as if you'd found it yourself."

Watch and tick what they do. Don't prompt.
- [ ] Played a card (tapped an answer)
- [ ] Read the reveal
- [ ] Pressed "Next question"
- [ ] Opened a full answer ("Read the full answer" / "Just tell me")
- [ ] Opened a fold (sources, "How big is that?", "What nobody knows yet")
- [ ] Used search. What did they type? ______
- [ ] Went to Today / Topics / The Earth
- [ ] Looked lost or stopped (where?) ______
- [ ] Wanted to leave (when?) ______

## 3. One task
> "Imagine a friend says: 'ChatGPT uses a bottle of water for every email.' Use the site to decide what to tell them."

- Did they find it? How (card, search, topics)? Time taken: ____
- What would they tell the friend? ______

## 4. Questions (2 to 3 minutes)
1. "What was the most surprising thing you saw?"
2. "Was anything confusing, or hard to believe?"
3. "Would you send any of this to someone? Which, and to whom?"
4. "Would you come back tomorrow for Today's question? Why or why not?"
5. "Do you trust these numbers? What made you trust them, or not?"
6. "What did you want to know that it didn't answer?"

## 5. Close
> "Thank you. That was really useful."

## What counts as a pass
- **7 of 10 can say what it's for** in their own words after 10 seconds (the spec's target).
- **Most play at least one card and open at least one answer.**
- **At least 3 name something they'd send.**
- The water task gets done in under a minute.
