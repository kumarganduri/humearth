## Affected Pages/Routes
- `/` Earth: static poster first, then the 3D toy globe. Check the promise, the single primary button, the ticker below the globe, and that tapping the ticker opens the tap panel.
- `/` My World (after the dive): about 3 s of world-only view, then the leaf/drop/sun glyphs and the sentence. The choice objects should toggle bloom.
- `/` How We Know: one reading column, a ranges table with sources, and the change log.
- `/#w1.<code>` friend's world: banner and "Make mine". A bad or old code should say "That world moved away".

## Key Interactions to Verify
- The quiz gives 3 one-at-a-time picture questions, the globe turns, and the last tap starts the dive.
- Tap or Esc during the dive lands straight in the world.
- Back to Earth runs the reverse dive.
- "Change my answers" from your own world pre-fills the quiz and keeps the same world layout (seed).
- Toggling the paintbrush, film reel and feather makes the world bloom.
- Share copies a `#w1.` link that holds the real world plus a "my plan" badge.
- The sound toggle is off by default and is remembered.

## Edge Cases
- A returning visitor opening a friend's link: their own world must stay unchanged.
- Private mode or blocked localStorage: the site still works, but the world isn't remembered.
- sites.json fails to load: the globe shows with no dots and a small note.
- No WebGL: a static illustration plus the numbers.
- WebGL context loss (tab switch on a phone): poster, then restore.
- A slow device (under 24 fps): crossfade and a lighter world.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: crossfades and no globe spin.
- A heavy text-only user stays healthy (≥ 0.8). A heavy video user is visibly stressed (< 0.5).

## Critical Paths
- First visit: Earth → quiz → dive → world → share.
- Returning visitor: Earth glow → "Visit my world" → world.
- Keyboard-only through the whole journey, with axe clean in each place.
